# Eric Rozán

Hi! I am Eric Rozán, a Ph.D. candidate in Physics at [Instituto Balseiro](https://www.ib.edu.ar/), Bariloche, Argentina.

My research focuses on mathematical modelling of epidemic processes, complex networks, and infectious disease dynamics.

[GitHub](https://github.com/ericrozan) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=D3r9jG4AAAAJ) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Eric-Rozan?ev=hdr_xprf) · [CV](./CV.pdf)
